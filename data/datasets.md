# Datasets Reference & Curation

NATURA integrates bioacoustic and visual taxonomies grounded in peer-reviewed biological datasets:

1. **Xeno-Canto Bioacoustic Archive**: Comprehensive avian acoustic corpus for spectral envelope verification.
2. **Insect Bioacoustics Archive (Orthoptera & Hymenoptera)**:
   - *Apis mellifera* (Honeybee) wingbeat fundamental: 230-260 Hz, harmonic series up to 1.2 kHz.
   - *Oecanthinae* (Tree Cricket) stridulation frequencies: 4.5 kHz - 7.2 kHz modulated by ambient temperature ($f = 0.08 \cdot T + 1.2$).
3. **Ultrasonic Bat & Insect Database**:
   - Echolocation sweeps: 25 kHz - 65 kHz down-converted using heterodyne demodulation.
4. **Plant Cavitation & Acoustic Emission**:
   - Xylem drought stress acoustic pulses (20 kHz - 100 kHz).
5. **iNaturalist Vision Taxonomy**: Morphological classification for flowering plants, pollinators, and songbirds.
