# vivo Office Kit Bridge Architecture

## Red Light / Green Light Workflow
- **Red Light (Phone-First)**:
  - Complete standalone execution directly on the iQOO 15.
  - Local SQLite database, on-device DSP, and mobile web dashboard.
- **Green Light (Laptop Collaboration)**:
  - vivo Office Kit bridges the iQOO 15 live stream to the laptop.
  - Laptop provides deep historical spectrogram analysis, soundscape stem exports, and citizen science bulk sync.
