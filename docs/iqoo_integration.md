# iQOO 15 Hardware Integration

NATURA is explicitly engineered to harness the hardware architecture of the **iQOO 15**:
- **Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 NPU**: Accelerates the multimodal embedding network with sub-10ms latency.
- **Triple 50MP Camera Matrix**: High optical resolution allows digital zoom crop on micro-pollinators without optical blur.
- **Stereo Audio Speakers**: Accurate stereo spatial rendering for generated soundscapes and sonified frequency panning.
- **7000 mAh Battery**: Supports sustained field observation sessions (up to 8 hours of continuous live multimodal analysis).
